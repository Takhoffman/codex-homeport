# v0.14.1

- Fixed Repair Shim App when Multihome is opened from Finder by locating the
  Homebrew `npx` executable outside the app's minimal inherited `PATH`.
- Repair failures now remain visible in the shim diagnostics card after the
  app refreshes bundle status, instead of being replaced by a generic stale or
  unpatched result.
- Added regression coverage for command discovery from Finder-launched apps.
