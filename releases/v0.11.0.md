# v0.11.0

- Reworked home and recent-launch cards around stable titles and launch actions,
  with readable, horizontally scrollable metadata pills and plain-language hover
  help.
- Moved temporary-home creation into a clear **Keep For** choice between saved
  and temporary homes, independent of the selected setup source.
- Added temporary variants of cloned, clean-room, and config-only homes while
  preserving their selected copy policies.
- Added independent Browser and Desktop App development launch controls and
  made their preference updates responsive and persistent.
- Improved linked-home diagnostics, background health checks, creation
  responsiveness, quit behavior, and terminal/provider launch handling.
